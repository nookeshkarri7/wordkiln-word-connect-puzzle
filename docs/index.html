About Wordkiln, the word game that starts on the puzzle

Wordkiln is a free word connect puzzle for Android. You swipe letters on a potter's wheel, the words glow into a crossword, and any real word you spell counts as a bonus.

Get it on Google Play Watch the trailer

Why players pick it

No maze before the game. One button takes you to your next level. No launch pop-ups, card packs or event timers.

Real words always count. A real word that isn't on the board pays 2 coins instead of being marked wrong.

Nothing to buy. Hints cost a fixed 25 coins earned by playing. Plays offline, no account.
